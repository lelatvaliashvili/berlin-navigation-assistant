# Barrier-Free Travel

BVG provides accessibility features and assistance for passengers with mobility,
visual, and hearing impairments.

## U-Bahn stations

According to the retrieved BVG information, approximately 86% of U-Bahn stations
can be reached without steps, and approximately 86% have a guidance system for
blind passengers.

These figures describe the network in aggregate. They do not establish the
current accessibility status of any particular station.

## Tram and bus stops

Approximately 73% of Berlin tram stops are described as barrier-free. A
barrier-free bus stop is at least 16 cm high and includes a guidance system for
blind passengers. Not all bus stops are barrier-free.

## Vehicles

All BVG buses are described as barrier-free. All tram lines use barrier-free
vehicles equipped with ramps and accessible space. Newer U-Bahn vehicles provide
ground-level accessibility; older vehicles provide ramps for accessible
boarding. Motorized ferries are also described as barrier-free.

## Assistance and mobility services

BVG provides information about mobility training for passengers with limited
mobility or vision. The VBB guide service provides free assistance for
passengers who prefer or require accompaniment when travelling by bus and train.

## Information accessibility

BVG uses a two-senses principle in passenger information: important information
should be both visible and audible where possible. This supports passengers with
visual or hearing impairments.
