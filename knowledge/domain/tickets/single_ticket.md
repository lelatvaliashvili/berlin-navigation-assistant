# Single Ticket

## Current fares

Fares reviewed against the official BVG source on 2026-08-23:

- Berlin AB: 4.00 EUR regular, 2.50 EUR reduced
- Berlin ABC: 5.00 EUR regular, 3.50 EUR reduced

The reviewed source does not list a Berlin BC price in its pricing table. No BC
fare may be stated or estimated from this document.

## Valid fare zones

Single tickets are available for Berlin fare zones AB, BC, and ABC.

Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER) is in fare zone C. An AB-only ticket does
not cover travel to or from BER; the ticket must include zone C, such as a
Berlin ABC ticket.

## Journey rules

A single ticket allows one journey in one direction. Transfers between eligible
public transport services are permitted during the journey. Round trips and
return journeys are not permitted.

## Validity period

A single ticket is valid for 120 minutes. The 120-minute validity period does
not override the restriction on round trips or return journeys.

## Reduced fare

Reduced fares are available for children aged 6 to 14, and for dogs.
Children under 6 may travel without an additional ticket.

## Items included without an additional ticket

Passengers may bring hand luggage, strollers, wheelchairs, and orthopedic aids
such as walking aids.
