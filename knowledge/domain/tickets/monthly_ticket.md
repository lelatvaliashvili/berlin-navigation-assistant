# Monthly Ticket

## Pricing

The reviewed source does not contain a monthly ticket price table. No monthly
ticket price may be stated or estimated from this document.

## Valid fare zones

Monthly tickets are available for Berlin fare zones AB, BC, and ABC.

## Journey rules

A monthly ticket permits unlimited journeys within the indicated fare zone.
The ticket is transferable.

## Validity period

The ticket is valid from the first day printed on the ticket until midnight at
the end of the final day printed on the ticket.

## Passenger take-along rules

The ticket holder may take along children under 6, one dog, hand luggage,
strollers, wheelchairs, and orthopedic aids.

From Monday to Friday, between 20:00 and 03:00 the following day, the holder may
take up to four additional people, of whom only one may be older than 14.

The same take-along allowance applies throughout Saturdays, Sundays, public
holidays, December 24, and December 31.

## Subscription

The monthly ticket may also be obtained as a subscription and issued as a VBB
fahrCard.
