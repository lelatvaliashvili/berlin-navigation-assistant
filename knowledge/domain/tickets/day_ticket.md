# 24-Hour Ticket

## Current fares

Fares reviewed against the official BVG source on 2026-08-23:

- Berlin AB: 11.20 EUR regular, 7.40 EUR reduced
- Berlin ABC: 12.90 EUR regular, 8.00 EUR reduced

The reviewed source does not list a Berlin BC price in its pricing table.

## Valid fare zones

The 24-hour ticket is available for Berlin fare zones AB, BC, and ABC.

## Journey rules

The ticket allows unlimited journeys within the selected fare zone during its
validity period.

## Validity period

The ticket is valid for 24 hours from the time of validation.

## Passenger take-along rules

A regular 24-hour ticket is valid for one adult. The ticket also allows the
holder to take along up to three children aged 6 to 14. Children under 6 may
travel without an additional ticket.

## Reduced fare

Reduced fares are available for children aged 6 to 14, and for dogs.

## Items and passengers included without an additional ticket

Hand luggage, strollers, wheelchairs, orthopedic aids such as walking aids,
one dog, and up to three children aged 6 to 14.
