# Multi-Ride Tickets

## 4-Trip Ticket

Fares recorded from the reviewed source:

- Berlin AB: 12.40 EUR regular, 7.40 EUR reduced
- Berlin ABC: 16.80 EUR regular, 11.60 EUR reduced

Travel validity: 4 trips in fare zone Berlin AB, BC or ABC. One journey in one
direction per trip. Changes are permitted. Round and return trips are not
permitted.

Validity period: 120 minutes in one direction, per trip.

Reduced fare applies to children aged 6 to 14 and to dogs.

Free to take along: children under 6, hand luggage, strollers, wheelchairs, and
other orthopedic aids such as walking aids.

## 4-Short-Trip Ticket

Fares recorded from the reviewed source:

- Berlin AB: 7.80 EUR regular, 6.20 EUR reduced

The reviewed source does not list a 4-short-trip ABC price.

Travel validity: 4 trips in the Berlin fare zones. Three stations by S-Bahn or
U-Bahn with changes permitted and without a stopover, or six stations on trams
with changes not permitted.

The ticket is generally valid for fare zone AB. Only if the start of the short
trip is in fare zone B is the ticket also valid for fare zone C.

Validity period: from validation, for the stated number of stations. With a
mobile phone ticket, 20 minutes for the stated number of stations.

Reduced fare applies to children aged 6 to 14 and to dogs.
