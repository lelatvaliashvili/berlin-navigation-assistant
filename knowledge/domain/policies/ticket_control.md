# Ticket Control and Increased Transport Charge

Passengers are required to hold a valid ticket when travelling on public
transport.

## Forgotten personalized subscription

A passenger who held a valid personalized BVG subscription but could not present
their fahrCard during an inspection has seven days to provide proof that they
owned a valid fahrCard at the time of inspection.

If the proof is accepted, the passenger pays a 7.00 EUR processing fee instead
of the 60.00 EUR increased transport charge.

Proof may be submitted through the BVG EBE online service.

## Travelling without a valid ticket

A passenger who did not hold a valid ticket at the time of inspection is
generally required to pay an increased transport charge of 60.00 EUR.

The BVG EBE service provides information about payment, including available
payment options.

## EBE receipt

Passengers who receive an increased transport charge are given an EBE receipt.
The EBE number printed on the receipt is required when managing or paying the
case through the relevant BVG service.
