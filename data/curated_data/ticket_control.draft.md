# Ticket Control

Everything you need to know about ticket controls and increased transportation charges (EBE)

According to the [VBB tariff regulations](/dam/jcr:d60ffbff-9055-4e95-8e62-fc707ab65b1e/vbb-tarifbroschuere.pdf) (only in German), every passenger is required to have a valid ticket. If you do not do this and are caught by our control personnel, you will be charged a so-called increased transportation fee, EBE for short.  
  
Now there are two possible scenarios:

1. **You have a personalized BVG subscription, but have forgotten your fahrCard.**  
   In this case, you have 7 days to prove that you were the owner of the fahrCard at the time of the ticket control and will only have to pay a processing fee of €7 instead of the €60 fine. In order to do this, you no longer need to visit our customer center, but can simply do so [online](https://www.bvg-ebe.de/).
2. **You did not have a valid ticket at the time of the ticket control - for whatever reason.**  
   In this case you have to bite the bullet and pay the full 60 € fine. You can now do this [online](https://www.bvg-ebe.de/)as well, and even pay in installments if necessary.

For more information on ticket controls and increased transportation charges, click [here](https://www.bvg-ebe.de/en/).

## EBE Receipt

If you are caught using public transportation without a valid ticket, you will receive this EBE receipt.   
In order to pay your increased transport fee, the "EBE number" (at the top) is especially important for you.
