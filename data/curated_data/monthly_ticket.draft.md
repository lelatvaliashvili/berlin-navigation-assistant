## Travel validity

- Tariff zone Berlin AB, BC, ABC
- Unlimited trips within the indicated fare zone
- Transferable

## Validity period

- From the first day printed on the ticket until midnight at the end of the last day printed on the ticket

## Subscription

As subscription:

- monthly debit payment or onetime, annual debit payment
- issued as a VBB fahrCard

[To our subscriptions](https://abo.bvg.de/de/subscriber/order)

## Free to take along

- Children under 6 years of age
- Hand luggage, buggies, wheelchairs and other orthopedic aids, e.g., walking aids
- 1 dog
- Plus up to four persons (but only one older than 14 years of age), Mon-Fri from 8 pm until 3 am on the next day, all day Sat, Sun, public holidays, and on 24/12 & 31/12

## Additional prices

- ABC + 1 state county: € 150
- ABC + 2 state counties oder 1 state county + 1 district-free city: € 186
- Full VBB network: € 225
